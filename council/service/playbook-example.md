CONTRACT PLAYBOOK of Corvina Analytics S.r.l. (fictional company), version 1.

How we review contracts. Flag a clause whenever it is there, whichever party it binds. Then recommend from our side.

Deal-breakers, recommend "do not sign" unless the counterparty removes them:
- uncapped liability on our side, or liquidated damages above 10% of the contract value;
- a non-compete or an exclusivity that binds us for more than 24 months or worldwide;
- an irrevocable or perpetual license of our own intellectual property.

Negotiate, recommend "negotiate":
- any covenant not to sue, even mutual: we keep our right to go to court on intellectual property;
- anti-assignment or change of control clauses that need the counterparty's consent for a merger of ours;
- minimum commitments and volume restrictions without a way out;
- most favored nation clauses, rights of first refusal or offer, no-solicit of employees or customers.

Acceptable, may sign: a cap on liability of at least 12 months of fees, termination for convenience with 60 days'
notice or more, non-disparagement if mutual, joint intellectual property ownership with a written split.
